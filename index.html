OAuth, without the mystery

One registration.
Many user connections.

An OAuth app identifies your integration to a provider. Each user then grants that app permission to access their own account.

See what each provider needs ↗
01

You register your integration

Add your app’s identity, a return address called a callback URL, and the permissions it needs. The provider gives you a client ID and, where needed, a secret.

02

The provider may review your app

This depends on the provider, the data you request, and who will use it. A marketplace listing or a customer’s admin approval can be a separate step.

03

Each user approves a connection

The user signs in with the provider and agrees to specific permissions. Your integration receives a token to act on those permissions, without receiving the user’s password.

Privacy and use notes +

This demo does not ask for provider passwords, OAuth client secrets, or access tokens. Checklist progress is stored in your browser. Research requests send the provider name and public website to this service and any configured research provider.

Share links may include the guide’s public source information. Avoid entering confidential information into the research form. Guides are planning aids: verify requirements with the provider before submitting your integration.
